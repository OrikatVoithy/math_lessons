Trigonometry · Level 2 of 6

Find a missing side

In Level 1 you learned what sin, cos and tan are. Now you'll use them to find a side you can't measure. Go through the 5 short steps one at a time. Each step ends with a quick question.

Step 1 of 5
  1. 1
    Quick recap

    Name the sides, then pick the ratio

    Find the angle θ first. The hypotenuse is the longest side, across from the square corner. The opposite is across from θ. The adjacent is next to θ.

    Then each ratio links two of those sides:

    • SOH: sin θ = opposite ÷ hypotenuse (جا = المقابل ÷ الوتر)
    • CAH: cos θ = adjacent ÷ hypotenuse (جتا = المجاور ÷ الوتر)
    • TOA: tan θ = opposite ÷ adjacent (ظا = المقابل ÷ المجاور)
  2. 2
    The key move

    Turn the equation around

    A 5 m ladder leans against a wall at 60° to the ground. How high does it reach? The height is opposite the angle and the ladder is the hypotenuse, so SOH gives:

    sin 60° = height ÷ 5

    The calculator says sin 60° = 0.866, so the question becomes 0.866 = ? ÷ 5. Which number, divided by 5, gives 0.866? To undo "divide by 5", multiply by 5: ? = 5 × 0.866 ≈ 4.3 m.

    The missing side can end up in one of two places in the fraction, and that tells you what to do:

    The ? is on top

    Multiply

    Ladder: sin 60° = ? ÷ 5

    ? = 5 × sin 60°

    = 5 × 0.866 ≈ 4.3 m

    The ? is on the bottom

    Divide

    A ramp rises 1 m at 10°. How long is it? sin 10° = 1 ÷ ?

    ? = 1 ÷ sin 10°

    = 1 ÷ 0.174 ≈ 5.8 m

    Remember it like this: ? on top, multiply. ? on the bottom, divide.

  3. 3
    A shortcut

    Cover-up triangles

    Some people find this picture easier. Put the top side of the ratio at the top of a triangle, with the ratio and the bottom side underneath. Cover the thing you want with a finger, and what's left tells you the sum.

    Example: you want the hypotenuse and you know the opposite, so use SOH. Cover H, and O sits over S, so H = O ÷ S. Side by side means multiply, one above the other means divide.

  4. 4
    How to solve

    A four-step recipe

    1. Name the sides from θ: hypotenuse, opposite, adjacent.
    2. Mark the side you know and the side you want.
    3. Pick the ratio that uses both of them: SOH, CAH or TOA.
    4. Write the equation and solve. ? on top: multiply. ? on the bottom: divide.
    Example: multiply

    The sun is 35° above the horizon. A tree's shadow is 12 m long. How tall is the tree?

    1. The tree is opposite the 35° angle. The shadow on the ground is adjacent.
    2. Know: adjacent = 12 m. Want: opposite.
    3. Opposite and adjacent: TOA, so use tan.
    4. tan 35° = ? ÷ 12. ? on top, so ? = 12 × 0.700 ≈ 8.4 m.
    Example: divide

    A ladder's foot is 1.5 m from a wall. The ladder makes 70° with the ground. How long is the ladder?

    1. The ladder is the hypotenuse. The 1.5 m along the ground is adjacent.
    2. Know: adjacent = 1.5 m. Want: hypotenuse.
    3. Adjacent and hypotenuse: CAH, so use cos.
    4. cos 70° = 1.5 ÷ ?. ? on the bottom, so ? = 1.5 ÷ 0.342 ≈ 4.4 m.
  5. 5
    Check your answer

    Does it make sense?

    • The hypotenuse is always the longest side. If your hypotenuse comes out shorter than another side, you probably multiplied when you should have divided, or the other way round.
    • Angles under 45° give a short opposite side. Below 45° the opposite is shorter than the adjacent, and above 45° it's longer. At exactly 45° they're equal.
    • Check the calculator is in degrees (DEG on the screen). Test it: sin 30 should give 0.5.
